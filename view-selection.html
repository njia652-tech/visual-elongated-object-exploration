View Selection Experiment

You will explore 32 three-dimensional objects across two tasks (64 trials total).
Use the arrow keys to rotate each object and confirm your chosen view.

Controls
  • ← →   Rotate left / right around the object (wraps 0–360°)
  • ↑ ↓   Tilt up / down around the object (max ±30°)
  • Enter   Confirm view (unlocks once you've rotated enough to inspect the object)
